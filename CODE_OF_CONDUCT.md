# Code of Conduct

Amika adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## In short

- Be welcoming, kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and levels of knowledge.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualised content, or publishing other people's
  private information.

This applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone representing the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/oscarandrespabonestrella). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may
warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does so, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
